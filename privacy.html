Tidaflip and your privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Tidaflip is made by Chairlurker LLC. This page says what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

The short version

The game asks before it sends anything. If you say no, nothing is sent. Either way, it never asks for your name, your email or an account.

Play data, if you say yes

When the game first starts, it asks whether it may send play data. If you agree, it sends:

  • A random ID for your copy of the game, so that sessions from the same copy can be read together. It isn't linked to your name or to any account, and a fresh one is made if you reset your progress.
  • A random ID for each play session, and the game's version.
  • What happens as you play: rounds finished, what you buy, sell, collect and unlock, the choices you make, the achievements and quests you complete, which windows you open, and how many flips and presses you make.
  • How long you have played and how long you were away.

Play data doesn't hold your name, your email, your location or details about your device.

Why

We use it to find where the game is confusing, too slow or too fast, and to fix that. We don't sell it, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it goes and how long it stays

Play data is stored in a database run for us by Supabase, in the United States. Each record is deleted automatically 12 months after it arrives.

Your choices

  • Stop sharing. Open Settings, then Data, and turn off "Share play data". Nothing is sent after that.
  • Delete what was sent. Open Settings, then Data, and press "Delete my play data". This removes the play data your copy of the game has sent.

Because play data carries no name or email, we can't find yours from an email address. The button in the game is the way to delete it.

Kept on your device

Your progress, your settings and your answer about play data are saved on your own device (in the browser's storage when you play on the web). They aren't sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them, including your progress.

The servers involved

The web version is served by GitHub, and play data is received by Supabase. Like every server on the internet, they see your IP address when your device connects, and they may keep it in their own logs for a while. It isn't added to the play data.

Children

Tidaflip isn't made for children under 13.

Questions

Write to privacy@chairlurker.com.
